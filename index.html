The Problem

Indian OPDs Don't Have Time to Type.

Doctors see 40–80+ patients a shift with 2–4 minutes each. Every minute spent writing notes is a minute taken from the patient — and real consultations aren't in clean English.

"दोन दिवस ताप आहे, Paracetamol 650 mg जेवणानंतर दोनदा घ्या"

Global dictation tools break on this kind of Hindi-Marathi-English code-switching. And India's DPDP Act makes cloud-based voice transcription a compliance risk most hospitals won't take.

The Solution

An AI That Listens, Understands, And Drafts.

CarePath runs entirely on-premise. It passively listens during consultation, uses AI to extract structured clinical data, and hands the doctor a pre-filled prescription to approve in one click. No typing. No dictation commands. No cloud.

How It Works

Five AI-Driven Steps

Ambient Listening

Captures natural consultation audio, zero commands needed.

Multilingual Transcription

Tuned for Hindi/Marathi/English code-switching + drug names.

Clinical Extraction

Pulls symptoms, denials, prescriptions, labs & advice.

Doctor Verification

Pre-filled screen — confirm or edit in seconds.

One-Click Output

Print-ready prescription + full audit trail.

What Makes It Different

Built For Real Indian Clinics.

Code-Switch Recognition

Handles the actual way doctors and patients speak — mixed language, mid-sentence.

Phonetic Drug Matching

Calibrated against Indian brand names like Dolo, Pan-D, Augmentin to prevent dangerous misreads.

Safety Gates

Blocks printing if dosage frequency or meal timing is incomplete.

On-Premise Processing

Patient voice data never leaves the clinic — fully DPDP compliant.

5-Category Extraction

Complaints, denials, prescriptions, diagnostics, and care advice — structured automatically.

Full Audit Trail

Transcript, AI draft, and final doctor edits logged for medicolegal traceability.
